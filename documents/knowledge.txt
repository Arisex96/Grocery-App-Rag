# INSTACART GROOCERY ASSISTANT KNOWLEDGE BASE

## Fruits & Vegetables Storage
- Bananas: Store at room temperature on a counter or hook. Do not refrigerate them, as the cold turns their skins black. Keep them away from other fruits to prevent fast ripening.
- Tomatoes: Store tomatoes stem-side down at room temperature. Refrigeration destroys their flavor and makes their texture mealy.
- Leafy Greens: Wash lettuce, spinach, and kale, shake off excess water, and wrap them loosely in paper towels. Store them in a plastic bag in the refrigerator's crisper drawer to keep them fresh and crisp for up to a week.
- Apples & Berries: Keep apples in a cool pantry or refrigerator. Berries should be stored in the refrigerator in a ventilated container and washed ONLY right before eating to prevent mold.
- Garlic, Onions & Potatoes: Store potatoes, onions, and garlic in a cool, dark, well-ventilated dry place (like a pantry basket). Do NOT store potatoes and onions together, as onions release gas that makes potatoes sprout faster.

## Cooking Ingredients & Basics
- Baking Essentials: All-purpose flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and vanilla extract are key essentials for baking recipes.
- Oils & Fats: Extra virgin olive oil is best for salad dressings and low-heat cooking. Use canola oil or vegetable oil for high-heat frying due to their high smoke points. Unsalted butter is preferred in baking for controlling salt contents.
- Spices: Main spices to keep in a pantry include salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, oregano, and chili powder.

## Fresh Grocery Categories
- Dairy & Eggs: Fresh milk, unsalted butter, cheddar cheese, Greek yogurt, large eggs, and whipping cream. Keep dairy items stored on middle shelves of the refrigerator where the temperature is most stable, not in the door.
- Bakery: Sliced white bread, whole wheat bread, sourdough bread, plain bagels, croissants, and blueberry muffins. Sourdough bread lasts longer due to its natural acidity.
- Meat & Seafood: Boneless chicken breasts, lean ground beef, fresh salmon fillets, and tail-on shrimp. Always store raw meats on the lowest shelf of the refrigerator in a tray to prevent juices from dripping onto other food.

## Simple Recipes

### Quick Fresh Tomato Pasta Recipe
- Ingredients:
  - 200g spaghetti or penne pasta
  - 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 400g fresh red tomatoes, diced
  - Fresh basil leaves
  - Salt and black pepper to taste
  - Grated Parmesan cheese
- Instructions:
  1. Boil water in a large pot with salt. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente, then drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the diced fresh tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer for about 10-15 minutes until tomatoes break down and create a sauce.
  4. Stir in the freshly drained pasta and fresh basil leaves. Sauté together for 1 minute.
  5. Serve hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese.

### Easy Garden Salad Recipe
- Ingredients:
  - 1 head of romaine lettuce, chopped
  - 1 cucumber, sliced
  - 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  - 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  - Salad dressing: 3 parts olive oil to 1 part lemon juice or vinegar, salt, pepper.
- Instructions:
  1. Wash and dry the lettuce, cucumber, and cherry tomatoes.
  2. Combine the chopped lettuce, cucumber slices, cherry tomato halves, and sliced red onion in a large salad bowl.
  3. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper together to create a light vinaigrette.
  4. Drizzle dress over the salad right before serving, tossing gently to coat all greens.
